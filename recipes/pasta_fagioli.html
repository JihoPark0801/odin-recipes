Pasta Fagioli

Description

Pasta e fagilio(pasta and beans) share the spotlight with aromatics and frest tomatoes in this traditional Italian soup

Ingredients

  • Oil
  • Vegetables
  • Garlic
  • Parsley
  • Red Pepper Flakes
  • Tomatoes
  • Pasta Noodles

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, parsley, and flakes. Cook and stir until ionino is translucent
  2. Stir in chicken broth,tomato sauce, and tomatoes. Reduce the heat to low
  3. Add pasta and cook until tender
  4. Stir un undrained beans and cook until heated
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
