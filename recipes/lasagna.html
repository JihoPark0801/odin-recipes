Description

This lasagna recipe is submitted by John Chandler and has ranked among the most popular lasgna recipes on the internet for two decades

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato
  • Sugar
  • Noodles

Steps

  1. Make the meat sauce
  2. Cook the noodles
  3. Make the ricotta mixture
  4. Layer the lasagna according to the recipe instruction
  5. Cover with foil and bake
  6. Let the lasagna rest before serving
